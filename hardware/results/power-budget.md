## What this does not prove
It does not prove actual leakage (PCB, flux, TVS), regulator ripple at the radio burst, inrush on slot switches, pack protection behaviour, cold-weather capacity, or Thread poll costs. Measure IT-08/IT-09 on Rev A (interface-test-matrix.md).
