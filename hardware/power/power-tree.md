# TL-SN-001 power tree and operating modes (H2)

Design: [HW-0003](../architecture/decisions/0003-integrated-power.md). Numbers: [`../results/power-budget.md`](../results/power-budget.md), generated by `hardware/scripts/power_budget.py`. Schematic: `hardware/kicad/TL-SN-001`, sections B and C.

```text
USB-C ─ CC 5.1k×2, USBLC6 ─ USB_VBUS ─┐
                                      ├─ TPS2121 (IN1 priority) ─ CHG_IN ─ BQ25186 ─ SYS_PATH ─ TPS63900 ─ V3V3_CORE
PV jack ─ SS34 ─ SMAJ12A ─ PV_IN ─────┘   (IN2 fallback, ST→EXP_B)       │  ↕ BAT        (3.3 V, CFG3=16.2k)
                                                                        │  protected 1S pack + NTC (TS/MR)
                                                       V3V3_CORE ─ 4× TPS22917 AON (default on)  → V3V3_AON_n
                                                       V3V3_CORE ─ 4× TPS22917 SW (default off, QOD) → V3V3_SW_n
```

## Operating-mode matrix

| Mode | Path | Expected behaviour | Risk / verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battery only | BAT → BQ25186 BATFET → SYS_PATH → TPS63900 | Boots and sleeps; about 24 µA deep sleep (model) | Below VBUVLO the charger cuts SYS. Check pack protection and firmware low-battery shedding |
| USB only, no battery | USB → TPS2121 IN1 → BQ25186 IN → SYS (4.5 V reg) | Runs normally. SYS is supplied from IN with an empty or missing battery *(src SLUSF69 §6.1)* | Charger sees no battery and may toggle detection. Bench IT-10 |
| USB + battery | USB powers SYS and charges, ICHG ≤ 300 mA (fw) | Unplugging USB switches SYS to the battery seamlessly *(src §6.1)* | Brown-out during the switch: IT-09 |
| PV + battery | PV → IN2 → BQ25186. VINDPM loads the panel and tracks VBAT | Charges by day, runs on the battery at night. ICHG ≤ 200 mA on PV (thermal) | Panel Voc must stay ≤ 12 V (TVS standoff) and below the 18.5 V OVP. Cloud transients |
| PV only, no battery | Same path | **Not supported**: it may brown out at dusk or in cloud. Firmware reports `power.source=pv_no_battery` as degraded | Document as refused, matching the handoff §20 |
| USB + PV + battery | TPS2121 selects USB (priority) and blocks reverse current into the panel | No cross-feed. ST tells firmware which source is active | PR1/CP2 divider values are TBD (HD-007) |
| Reverse PV polarity | SS34 blocks | No damage within the diode/TVS rating | TVS orientation review item |
| Over-voltage PV | SMAJ12A clamps; TPS2121 24 V abs max; BQ25186 OVP 18.5 V | Survives within the TVS energy rating | Size the TVS to the panel's short-circuit current |
| Deeply discharged battery | BQ25186 precharge | Precharge or refuse | NTC cold and hot thresholds vs chemistry (HD-006) |
| Pack removed while running | On USB/PV: keeps running. On battery only: off | As designed. Not promised uninterrupted | – |
| Radio TX burst | 123 mA at 3V3 *(src)* → about 160 mA from the battery at 3.0 V | TPS63900 >400 mA *(src)*; 22 µF output | Measure the droop at the module pin |

## Firmware obligations (feed `firmware/portable` power admission)
1. Read PMUX_ST and CHG_PG_N from EXP_B, and BQ25186 status over I²C, to publish `PowerStatus` with the source, charging state and faults. Never infer "battery healthy" from the absence of a fault.
2. Set ICHG according to the source (≤ 200 mA on PV, ≤ 300 mA on USB), and set a VINDPM mode that suits the panel.
3. On battery: keep SW rails off except during sampling, and refuse optional high-power tasks (admission control already exists in `firmware/portable`).
4. Enable the VBAT ADC reading only at low duty: the divider is high-impedance and the 100 nF hold capacitor needs about 1 s to settle after reset.

## Alternatives kept on file
A switching charger (BQ25620 class) for larger panels, a regulated-USB solar module (no board change), and an ideal-diode controller instead of the SS34 (Rev B).
