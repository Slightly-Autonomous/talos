| P1 | TL-SN-002 USB-C attack surface (red team 2026-11-21; owner kept the port outside, 2026-11-22) | **Mitigated in firmware and config:** (1) the diag console is read-only while the lid is closed (`console_policy.h`); every mutating command needs the lid open, which raises the P0 tamper alarm, and refusals reach the hub as a diagnostic (health DEGRADED on repeated attempts; twin scenario `garage_usb_console_attack`). (2) Firmware lamps are lit only with the lid open or in service mode, so a power bank on the port reveals nothing but the hardware PWR lamp. (3) The production ESP-IDF profile (`sdkconfig.production`, CI compile-checked) enables Secure Boot v2, release-mode flash encryption, JTAG off and secure ROM download mode, with external signing and no key in the repo. **Residual:** the PWR lamp shows that USB power is present; the owner must run provisioning (`docs/hardware/provisioning.md`), and the first flash burns eFuses irreversibly; NVS encryption (HMAC eFuse key) and anti-rollback are now in the production profile (compile-checked); nothing is bench-verified yet | Owner provisioning; bench (HD-016) |
| P1 | TL-SN-002 residual risks (red team 2026-11-21) | Accepted: (1) a fixed sensor whose wake line is held high 10 min (someone loitering at the PIR, a magnet at the Hall) is quarantined and retried after 1 h; the first motion and a P0 trouble report were already sent (TL-SN-001 only masks the wake line). (2) A sensor that starts but then times out (e.g. a PIR that never settles) restarts its backoff at 1 h each cycle: one report per hour, not 24 h. (3) The hub shows low AA cells as LOW_POWER only on battery; on USB the mode stays normal, so cells that are low but not missing (0 mV, now DEGRADED) show only on the node's BATT lamp until USB is unplugged; the hub has no board manifests to hold the threshold. (4) A held USER button is masked as a wake source; its release is seen at the next wake (long-press mark or heartbeat). (5) "commissioning requested" stays in the device's health evidence until the hub restarts | Bench + provisioning |

| P1 | Tamper/maintenance residual risks | Accepted after the 2026-11-21 red team (ADR 0007 amendment (b)): (1) an owner who starts maintenance after a lid opened but before its report arrives (node asleep or offline) suppresses that opening; if the lid is closed before the window ends, nothing alarms (humans only, journaled as `AlarmSuppressed`). (2) A first-ever `tamper.state` open (hub never knew the state, e.g. commissioning) does not alarm; the device shows FAULTED. (3) A flood of tamper openings from one node makes one alarm command each: no cooldown yet (ADR 0007 deferred). (4) Late openings of other kinds (contact, motion) are still history only, exactly the P0-overtaken-by-P1 loss fixed for tamper; extending the exception is a rules decision. (5) Event-id ownership is a prefix check: a device id that is another's prefix plus ":" (e.g. `a` and `a:b`) could still share ids; device ids are owner-authored. (6) Toggling external power (PV cable) can hold EXP_WAKE asserted; the node then polls at 1 Hz (detection still within ~1 s) at a battery cost. (7) The BOOT service button and its LEDs are inside the enclosure, so using them means the lid is open (alarm); service mode is bounded to 10 min and shows only card state. (8) The hub's maintenance expiry follows the hub's wall clock; a clock step back lengthens a window. (9) The UI's remaining-time label uses the browser clock; the dot uses the hub's health verdict | Revisit with rule cooldowns, provisioning (unique device ids) and the supervised loop |
